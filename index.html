How we work

A Florida company, a licensed kitchen

Guacamole Station LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. Food is prepared in a licensed and inspected kitchen.

We make no health or dietary claims about anything we serve. Allergen information is available on request, and because the kitchen handles common allergens including gluten, dairy and nuts, nothing can be promised free of traces. Tell staff about an allergy before ordering and they'll give you a straight answer.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Where are you and when are you open?

[LOCATION ADDRESS] — [YOUR OPENING HOURS]. Call [PHONE NUMBER] to check before travelling.

How do I order ahead?

[PHONE NUMBER OR ORDERING LINK]. Small orders need [MINUTES] notice; group orders need [HOURS].

Can you do office lunches?

Yes, that's a lot of what we do. Send the headcount, the time and the address and we'll quote in writing, free.

Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes, and they're built the same way at the counter. For group orders tell us the numbers so we plan rather than improvise.

How do you handle allergies?

Speak to staff before ordering. We'll tell you what's in each item, and we're honest that a shared kitchen can't guarantee zero traces.

How much food do I need for twenty people?

Ask us — we'll tell you honestly, and it's usually less than you think. Over-ordering is the most common mistake with trays.

Feeding a team this week?

Send the headcount and the time. A written quote, free, within one business day.
