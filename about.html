Why the counter is in front of you

You can see every ingredient, ask what it is, and watch it go in. That answers most questions before they're asked.

How we work

Visible, made to order, on time

Fast-casual works when the food is assembled when you order it. The speed comes from preparation behind the scenes, not from food sitting ready in trays.

Being able to see everything matters more than people expect, particularly to anyone avoiding an ingredient. Staff can point at what's in a dish instead of reading from a card.

For offices, timing is the whole service. Lunch that arrives fifteen minutes into a meeting has failed, however good it is, so delivery times are confirmed in writing and then met.
